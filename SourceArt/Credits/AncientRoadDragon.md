# Ancient Road Dragon preview asset

The dragon mesh, skeleton, idle animation and normal map come from [Black Dragon with Idle Animation by 3DHaupt](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fb0053a2e59b43868e934c239bf4eb36), licensed [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). This Unity arena uses it as a local prototype asset. Commercial use would require permission from the creator or a replacement.

The Unity derivative removes the source stage and lamps, exports the dragon mesh with its skeleton and idle only, keeps the source normal map, and remaps the color texture to jade and bronze for continuity with the DiceBound Ancient Road Dragon. The path, arena geometry, and materials outside the dragon itself were authored in DiceFree.
