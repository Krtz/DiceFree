# Idea Inbox

This is the low-friction dumping ground for DiceFree ideas that are **not yet decisions** and may not deserve their own issue yet.

Put half-formed ideas here rather than losing them in chat.

When an idea becomes concrete:
- turn implementation/design work into a GitHub Issue;
- move settled canon/rules into the relevant design document;
- record major settled choices in `DECISION_LOG.md`;
- delete or mark the inbox note as promoted.

## Current loose ideas

- The six outer faces correspond thematically to DiceBound's six boards.
- The edges and corners of the Dice may be special locations rather than ordinary region terrain.
- The question of what the Dice itself is resting/floating in should remain a long-term mystery.
- Class advancement should visibly escalate the character, not only change numbers and spell names.
- Hidden advancement paths can be unlocked through unusual achievements, bosses, items, locations or account discoveries.
- Important Mythical/Artifact gear should deserve bespoke visual identity on the character.
- Earlier regions should remain relevant in later difficulty/endgame rather than become disposable leveling zones.
