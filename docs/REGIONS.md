# Regions

DiceFree is planned around **six major regions**, corresponding to the six outer faces of the Dice.

## Region rule

Each region should be inspired by the identity of its matching DiceBound board, but translated into a full 3D RPG region with its own culture, ecology, dungeons and progression.

## Working structure for each region

- Regional capital or hub
- 2–4 major overworld sub-zones
- Smaller settlements/outposts where appropriate
- Multiple quest chains
- Advancement quests
- 2+ normal dungeons over time
- One major capstone dungeon/raid-like encounter
- Named elites and secret bosses
- Region-specific item sets and artifacts
- Region-specific pets/cosmetics/discoveries
- A major clue about the Dice

## Progression

The six faces do not have to be six strictly linear levels forever. Initial progression may introduce them in sequence while later difficulties and endgame systems make earlier faces relevant again.
