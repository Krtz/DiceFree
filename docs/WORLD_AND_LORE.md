# World and Lore

## Foundational premise

DiceBound is the inside of the Dice.

At the end of DiceBound, the player defeats **The Last Equation** and breaches the boundary of the world they believed was everything.

DiceFree begins on the **outside of the Dice**.

The exact cosmology is intentionally not fully defined yet. The central image is a massive cube-world or planet-like object whose exterior has six habitable faces.

## The six faces

Each face is a major region and should be thematically related to one of DiceBound's six boards without simply copying it.

The connection can be physical, metaphysical, historical or environmental. Internal events may have shaped the corresponding exterior face.

Each face should eventually have:
- a strong visual identity;
- one or more settlements;
- overworld sub-zones;
- its own enemy families;
- class/advancement content;
- dungeons;
- world bosses or major encounters;
- secrets that deepen the Dice mystery.

## The Last Equation

The Last Equation is the narrative bridge between games.

Open questions:
- Was it a jailer, law of reality, defense mechanism, god-like intelligence, or something else?
- Did defeating it free the player, damage the Dice, or merely open a path?
- Did anyone on the outside know life existed inside?
- Is the Dice artificial?
- What is the Dice sitting in/on?
- Are there other Dice?

These should remain open until the wider cosmology earns an answer.

## Tone

The world can be sincere and mysterious without losing DiceBound's capacity for absurdity. Serious ancient ruins and horrifying cosmic truths can coexist with bizarre classes, strange loot and NPCs with personality.
