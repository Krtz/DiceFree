## Practical shortlist

1. **Medieval Man** — 10,000 triangles, one material; the most straightforward new generic NPC rigging candidate. The matching EmanuelP listing specifies CC BY 4.0. It has no packaged rig or clips.
2. **Low Poly Owl Warrior / Winged Lion Warrior / Low Poly Monster Dragon** — about 19,000 triangles each and one material; more plausible creature/character prototype sources than the million-triangle models. All need rigging and animation; matching iRahulRajput listings specify CC BY 4.0.
3. **Knight with Spear and Shield** — ~44,175 triangles; matching mykola listing specifies CC BY 4.0. Thirteen mesh geometries/materials and no skin: useful modelling source, not a ready class replacement.
4. **Medieval House (1)** — ~2,148 triangles and one material; technically the simpler of the two houses. Keep on hold until its exact creator/license and texture budget are verified. The 107.8 MiB ZIP size shows that low geometry does not imply small textures.
5. **Medieval House** — ~23,700 triangles, three mesh geometries including a door, ten materials; source unresolved.
6. **Mountain Dragon** — ~20,460 triangles, 95 joints, one skin and one animation. **Hold:** the Alexey listing's CC BY claim needs upstream verification. The packaged filename and triangle count also match PROTOFACTOR's older `MOUNTAIN DRAGON ANIMATIONS` commercial listing. This is a provenance lead, not a finding of infringement.
7. **Duat Golem** — ~39,738 triangles, 57 joints and one animation, but **hold as third-party-series content**, with uploader attribution to another modeller. Do not treat its Free Standard label as proof of underlying character rights.

The well, Abyss Walker and both plague doctors are roughly 1.9–2.0 million triangles without rigs. They are appearance/retopology references rather than immediate game-ready models.

## Repeat the inspection

```powershell
python tools/assets/inspect_downloaded_assets.py --source T:\TEMP\ART_ASSETS --output docs/assets/downloaded-assets-2026-10-15.json
```

The scanner reads archive/model data without importing to Unity or executing packaged scripts. Nested RAR inspection uses libarchive `tar` if available, with explicit unsupported-decoder errors. Maximum member size is 768 MiB; nesting is bounded to three ZIP levels; GLB JSON is bounded to 64 MiB. It does not extract files into the source folder. `game-dev` is unavailable on this PC, so no package admission or lock receipt is claimed.

Before integration: resolve source/license and required credits, inspect visible materials/UVs/scale, evaluate topology/rig/animations, prepare LODs and texture budgets, then perform an isolated engine import. Do not commit the multi-gigabyte raw download folder or silently replace class/NPC/boss models as part of catalog maintenance.
