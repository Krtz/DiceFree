- **Medieval House (1):** ~2,148 triangles, one material; source now identified as unrealmahin, CC BY 4.0.
- **Medieval House:** ~23,700 triangles, ten materials; The linked source page lists CC BY 4.0.

The high-detail Pigcraft undead/fungal models are around 1.9-2.0 million triangles
without rigs. Optimization and animation work are still required. FBX/OBJ counts
are polygon-fan estimates, not evaluated engine costs; animation curves alone do
not validate visual quality or a complete gameplay animation set.

No new engine import or runtime credit entry was made by the license audit.
