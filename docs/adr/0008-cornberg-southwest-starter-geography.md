# ADR 0008: Cornberg Southwest and Slime Forest Starter Geography

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-08
- Scope: DiceFree

## Decision

Decision: Cornberg is located near the southwest corner of the first cube face. The first face extends considerably northeast beyond the starter village. The world should open outward naturally from the mountain/village starting point, along a road into larger slime forest and toward the dungeon exterior.

The starter landscape contains multiple crop fields, one or more crop slimes per field, a varied larger woodland with greater slime population, a readable mountain advancement landmark and an accessible road network. Keep quests, marked areas, NavMesh, saves, NPC roles and combat functional while changing the scene; do not regenerate the mature scene from the old phase-one blockout. The cube-face transition remains as in ADR 0006.

Do not design or implement dungeon interior until the user collaborates on that design.

## Consequences

This is a stable design decision. Implementation can evolve, but changes to this policy require a superseding ADR; granular art tuning, individual room details and ordinary balance knobs remain in the design documents.
