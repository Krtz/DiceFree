# ADR 0009: Fantasy Character Art and Flexible Environment Realism

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-08
- Scope: DiceFree

## Decision

Decision: classes, playable forms, NPCs, monsters, equipment and important spell effects establish DiceFree's stylized exaggerated fantasy identity, following the readability/silhouette goals of Warcraft III ORPG and Magicka while remaining original. Environmental and background assets may be more realistic when appropriate; realism in cliffs, ground, rocks, forest backgrounds or mountains is acceptable and is NOT a style violation.

Author actual Blender 3D source and game-engine exports for runtime assets. Maintain readable isometric silhouettes, logical collisions and tint/recolorable materials. The source model and the Unity import/prefab are distinct deliverables; 2D reference pictures are not replacements for 3D implementation.

## Consequences

This is a stable design decision. Implementation can evolve, but changes to this policy require a superseding ADR; granular art tuning, individual room details and ordinary balance knobs remain in the design documents.
