# Anvilreach and the southern World Two biomes

Implemented on `feat/verdant-wilds` for the request to add a large mining and
metalworking city and more World Two biomes. Names and layout are implementation
choices for this request.

## Anvilreach

The city occupies local X 30–170 / Z -282–-136, east of the central Crown road.
Its centre is X 92 / Z -205. Basalt Needle and Ember Crown retain their existing
positions and scale as the world's two pips.

- Twenty-six buildings: miners' guildhall, Copper Kettle inn, exchange, foundry,
  smiths' cooperative, assayer, workshops, storehouses, healer, barracks and homes.
- Three open masonry gates, four watchtowers, paved avenues and exchange plaza.
  Western and northern branches join the existing Crown road; the southern gate
  reconnects to the central route.
- Three metalworkers' stalls, outdoor workbenches, anvils, carts and metal bars.
- Three furnace presentations with glowing hearths, flickering lights, chimneys
  and smoke. Street lights share the existing day/night clock.
- A timber-supported mine gallery, ore tram rails, mineral outcrops, supply
  barrels and mine foreman establish the mining setting.

Master smith Branna offers the ordinary merchant interaction and existing shop
supplies. Anvilreach Banker opens the shared Echo bank. The central spring heals
125 base HP/second. The new resurrection dice uses `anchor.anvilreach` and the
existing approach-to-activate system. Nine residents have conversation/services.
All service approaches are outside building footprints.

This adds a functioning settlement and industrial setting. It does not add ore
currency, harvesting professions, crafting recipes or an instanced mine dungeon.

## Three more biomes

| Region | Location and appearance |
| --- | --- |
| Coppervein Highlands | Central/eastern hills around the city: oxidised soil, mineral seams, quarry stone and ore workings. |
| Glasswind Barrens | Southeast: pale ash and dark volcanic glass. Previous woodland is cleared from the barren region. |
| Deepmoss Fen | Southwest: moss-dark soil, teal woodland, luminous mushrooms and small dark pools. |

Ashfall Reach and Steamroot Vale remain, giving World Two five biomes. Ground
colours blend across region edges. City/biome names appear on the minimap; four
new destinations and city roads appear on the atlas. The rotating cube receives
a refreshed World Two photograph.

Existing towns, both pips, river, bridges and the Verdant Order expedition remain.
Four patrol homes were moved away from the city; enemy definitions and rewards
are unchanged. The dragon's origin remains unresolved. No World Three crossing
is added.

## Assets and authoring

The local catalog was searched for village, metalworking, container and nature
sources. This pass reuses already imported/tracked `VisualBatch01` anvils, carts,
crates, barrels, rocks, pines and mushrooms; `WalkingVillagers` residents; existing
handcrafted architecture, materials, spring and dice presentations. No new raw
Asset Store package is added to Git. New masonry, paving, furnace, market and
mine-support shapes and procedural textures are generated by the authoring code.

`tools/authoring/WorldTwoIndustryAuthoring.cs` extends the existing Ashfall
authoring partial. From the preceding World Two scene, run `BuildIndustry`,
`PolishIndustry`, then `FinishIndustry` in an Editor adapter containing the other
authoring partials. The initial pass rejects duplicate city creation.
`RefineIndustry` rebakes navigation and validates city services and existing
roads. `RepairIndustryPalette` independently refreshes terrain colours. Its input
is a native Texture2D asset, copied through a RenderTexture rather than decoded
as image bytes. Capture the World Two photograph through
`CaptureWorldFaceTexture.cs`, run `WorldFacePreviewAuthoring.Batch`, then rebuild.

Runtime changes cover region/map descriptions and foundry lighting. Roads use
the existing movement-speed surface. Wall, building and work-prop footprints
participate in the full World Two navigation bake.
