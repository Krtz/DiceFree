## Separate provenance and integration notes

These notes do not replace or rename the page licenses listed above.

- **Black Dragon:** original 3DHaupt page lists CC BY-NC 4.0. Other uploads of similar geometry claim CC BY; the original source is recorded here.
- **Warrior Girl:** NORBERTO-3D's page and embedded GLB license agree on CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. ZIP and GLB count as one model.
- **Kindred, HIT2 models and Duat:** pages have recorded licenses, while the named external game/series designs still need rights review.
- **Cat Witch:** AKM's page credits Waspsalad's Minecraft witch reinterpretation.
- **Mushroom Warrior / Truffle Man:** source pages credit external concept artists; preserve those provenance notes.
- **Medieval Civilian 3 / Skeleton Animated:** source descriptions mention Adobe Fuse/Mixamo and a Unity Asset Store model respectively.
- **Mountain Dragon:** retain the earlier possible PROTOFACTOR upstream-source lead for review.
- High triangle counts, missing rigs, RAR decoder limitations and Blender compatibility are technical readiness matters. They do not make a page's stated license unknown.

No model imports, class changes or runtime credit entries were made by this audit.
