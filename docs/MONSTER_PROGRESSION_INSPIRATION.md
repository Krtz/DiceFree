# Monster progression inspiration (nonbinding)
Status: **reference / brainstorming only**. Not approved encounter levels, unlocks, new regions or world lore.

## Classic fantasy monster ladder
User-supplied Google research, preserved as a rough genre reference (not a rigid DiceFree scaling prescription):

| Illustrative level bracket | Familiar enemies and gameplay lessons |
| --- | --- |
| 1–5, tiny pests | Green slimes, giant rats/bugs, weak imps or goblins; establish basic attacks, movement and target selection |
| 6–15, wilderness | Wolves, boars, bandits, zombies, skeletons; faster threats, packs, positioning, simple weapons and magical interactions |
| 16–30, mythological | Orcs, ogres, trolls, harpies, gargoyles, lizardfolk and gnolls; bigger threats, mixed enemy roles, crowd control and resistances |
| 31–50, exotic/elemental | Elementals, golems, basilisks, manticores, griffins, chimeras; environmental themes, resistances and condition effects |
| 51+, major threats | Demons, dragons, eldritch enemies and godlike enemies; advanced cooperative mechanics and strong visual escalation |

## Efficient visual families
A family can support multiple regions using model scaling, recolored materials, equipment/attachments, distinctive silhouettes and **different mechanics** (not just bigger HP).
Examples from the user-provided inspiration: green -> red/burning -> rare gold/reward-rich slimes; gray -> dire -> frost wolves. These are **examples only**, not proposed DiceFree spawn tables.

## DiceFree interpretation
- Current approved first region remains Cornberg-side slime forest, with the first Slime Dungeon under construction. Do not retcon its accepted encounters or dungeon-level-50 secret route based on the illustrative ladder.
- DiceFree advancement resets levels at milestones; the quoted level brackets should **not** be transplanted literally into DiceFree progression.
